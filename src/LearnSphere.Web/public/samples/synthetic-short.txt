Data Privacy Basics: Corporate Training

Personal data is any information that identifies a person directly or indirectly. Collect only the personal data needed for the agreed business purpose. Explain the purpose before collecting employee information.

Use approved encrypted storage for confidential records. Restrict access to colleagues who need the records for their work. Review access permissions every quarter and remove access when a colleague changes roles.

Report a suspected data breach immediately to the security team. Preserve evidence and avoid deleting suspicious messages. The response team records the discovery time and coordinates the investigation.

Verify the recipient before sending a sensitive attachment. Use the approved secure transfer tool instead of personal email. An accidental disclosure should be reported even when it was unintentional.

Follow the retention schedule for each record category. Delete records securely when their approved retention period ends. A legal hold pauses routine deletion until the legal team releases it.

Use a unique password for each work account. Enable multi-factor authentication wherever it is available. Never share an authentication code with someone who calls unexpectedly.

A manager must review generated training material before sharing it with employees. A matching source excerpt does not prove that a quiz answer is correct. Human review should check context and whether each distractor is plausible.
